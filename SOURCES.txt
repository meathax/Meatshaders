V4 numerical derivations
------------------------
Easymode:
  - canonical Lanczos-family H with SHARPNESS_H squared as in source;
  - direct phase/RGB fit to MiSTer max-RGB adaptive interpolation;
  - source cutoff vertical interpolation with SHARPNESS_V=1;
  - DC-constrained binary-neighbourhood fit for optional anti-ringing H;
  - exact shadowmask.sv shift/add/truncate model for paired gain fitting.

Lottes:
  - source-default Tri + bloomAmount*Bloom kernel with DO_BLOOM enabled;
  - constrained rank-one four-tap fit of H3/H5/H7 and Scan/BloomScan;
  - exact piecewise sRGB target evaluation for matched V/mask fitting;
  - exact shadowmask.sv arithmetic for 47e/27e/17e calibration.

All stored tables are sampled at phase i/256 and quantised to 1/256. Conjugate
phase rows are emitted as exact tap reversals; phase 128 is self-symmetric.


Port status
-----------
The coefficient tables are numerical fixed-pipeline derivatives of the named
shader kernels. Easymode-derived material retains the original shader's GPL
licensing status. Lottes' source explicitly permits unrestricted reuse.

No claim is made that MiSTer's fixed pipeline executes or fully reproduces the
original shader programs. See README.txt and AUDIT_REPORT.txt for limitations.
