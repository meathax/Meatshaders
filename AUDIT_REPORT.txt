====================================================================
 MiSTer CRT CANONICAL 1080p PACK - PIXEL-ACCURACY AUDIT
 2026-07-17; Guest re-audit and expansion 2026-07-21
====================================================================

AUDIT SCOPE
-----------
This audit covers exactly seven shader families and fourteen presets: one bare
landscape/default preset and one "- TATE" preset per family. The runtime pack
contains 20 filters, six gamma LUTs and seven masks. No other preset contract
is part of the release.

  CRT Easymode / CRT Easymode - TATE
  CRT Guest Advanced / CRT Guest Advanced - TATE
  CRT Guest Advanced Fast / CRT Guest Advanced Fast - TATE
  CRT Guest Advanced Fastest / CRT Guest Advanced Fastest - TATE
  CRT Lottes / CRT Lottes - TATE
  CRT Royale / CRT Royale - TATE
  CRT Royale Kurozumi / CRT Royale Kurozumi - TATE

The target is specifically 1920x1080 HDMI output, mask mode 1x, with no
overscan or display-side resampling. Every family except Lottes requires
adaptive filter interface v7. Lottes deliberately uses a fixed H/V pair and
gamma off.


REFERENCE AND HARDWARE MODEL
----------------------------
Shader math is evaluated from the pinned reference modules in tools/targets/.
The MiSTer side models the audited scaler and mask RTL rather than idealized
floating-point convolution:

  - 12-bit scaler phase is truncated to its upper eight bits;
  - 256 signed 10-bit coefficient rows are applied with intermediate clamps;
  - adaptive A/B coefficients use the RTL's single signed shift after the
    weighted sum, preserving valid odd coefficients;
  - adaptive control is the maximum gamma-LUT RGB channel;
  - H, V and mask stages saturate in hardware order;
  - v2 mask tokens use exact shift/add/truncate arithmetic;
  - channel-aware LUTs are retained for Kurozumi.

All gamma searches include black, near-black and codes 253, 254 and 255. Mask
errors are evaluated over the complete least-common-multiple supercell, not a
convenient crop. Both best rigid phase alignment and strict output-pixel origin
are measured; release masks must satisfy the strict origin contract.


MEASURED DEFAULT FIDELITY
-------------------------
Endpoint-inclusive end-to-end RMS is measured through H, V, gamma and mask in
8-bit output codes over the full periodic target. Lower is closer.

  CRT Guest Advanced:       1.237 codes RMS
  CRT Guest Advanced Fast:  1.237 codes RMS (identical tables; see below)
  CRT Guest Advanced Fastest: 1.160 codes RMS
  CRT Royale:              19.968 codes RMS
  CRT Royale Kurozumi:     19.854 codes RMS
  CRT Easymode:             3.591 codes RMS
  CRT Lottes:               1.166 codes RMS

Royale includes the measured finite-subline integration needed to keep its
narrow source beam stable at 240p-to-1080p. This raises its source-shape RMS
from the unconstrained fit, but removes the visible integer-line beat while
remaining subject to the same clipping and full-period gates as every family.

These values are approximation error against the source shader, not noise in
the output. Higher values for Royale and Kurozumi primarily expose structures
that MiSTer's four shared taps and v2 tokens cannot encode: wide bloom, large
slot geometry, independent phosphor-channel multipliers and per-channel beam
control.


EXACT 1080p STABILITY TEST
--------------------------
Line stability is simulated across complete 1080-line frames at:

  240 source lines: scale = 1080/240 = 4.5
  224 source lines: scale = 1080/224 = 4.821428571...

Input levels 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% are evaluated. The larger of peak-line and
trough-line standard deviation is gated at 0.03*255 = 7.65 codes. Using a full
frame is important: a short approximate scale can hide or exaggerate the beat
period.

  Family                    224p -> 1080p    240p -> 1080p
  CRT Easymode                    3.13             5.50
  CRT Guest Advanced              2.69             4.50
  CRT Guest Advanced Fast         2.69             4.50
  CRT Guest Advanced Fastest      2.81             5.50
  CRT Lottes                      1.73             3.50
  CRT Royale                      3.98             7.00
  CRT Royale Kurozumi             6.65             6.00


FITTING AND ACCEPTANCE GATES
----------------------------
For the four adaptive families, fitting alternates the transfer LUT and dark /
bright V endpoints against the integer model. A candidate is adopted only when
the exact end-to-end score improves by the configured margin and the
flat-field clipping invariant remains satisfied. The final mask is then
refitted against the complete pipeline, followed by one mask-aware LUT pass
with the same self-gate.

Every release build checks:

  1. Filter, gamma, mask and preset syntax.
  2. Exact inventory: 20 filters, 6 gamma LUTs, 7 masks, 14 presets.
  3. Exact case-sensitive references from every preset.
  4. Bare-default and TATE axis contracts.
  5. Gamma monotonicity, endpoint coverage, plateau and step behavior.
  6. Flat-field clip safety through maximum-RGB adaptive control.
  7. Full-supercell RMS and worst individual-pixel error.
  8. Strict-origin mask error and mask periodicity.
  9. Full-frame 224p/240p-to-1080p peak/trough stability.
 10. RGB ramps, saturated colors, impulses, steps and checker patterns.


PRESET AND INTERNAL-STAGE CONTRACTS
-----------------------------------
For every family except Lottes, the landscape preset selects:

  H         = family H filter
  V         = family adaptive V filter
  interlace = family no-scanline V filter
  gamma     = family LUT
  mask      = family mask
  maskmode  = 1x

TATE transposes H and adaptive V, uses the fixed H table for interlace, retains
the same LUT/mask, and selects `maskmode=1x rotated`.

Lottes uses its fixed H/V pair in both orientations, gamma off and the same
strict-origin mask. These filter files are internal scaler stages; they do not
represent additional user-facing presets.


GUEST RE-AUDIT 2026-07-21
-------------------------
The three Guest families are built from guest.r's upstream release drop
crt-guest-advanced-2026-07-12-release1, which supersedes the libretro pin as
the pack's source of truth for Guest. The release's advanced chain was
diffed pass by pass against the pinned libretro revision: at default
parameters the two are numerically identical (the new `ssharp` and
`bloomsamp` parameters default off, and the glow/bloom sigma rescales are
exactly 1 at default radii).

The re-audit found and fixed one real defect in the vendored reference:
the shader's dark_compensate expression clamps mix(mcut, maskstr, mx) to
[0, 1] BEFORE subtracting 1, so for the default CGWG mask (mask_compensate
= fract(2.0) = 0) it is identically 1.0. The previous reference dropped the
upper clamp and modelled a near-black boost of up to 10%, biasing the fit
target below input code ~58. After the correction and refit, near-black
(codes 0-60) masked RMS improved from 0.993 to 0.916 codes (worst pixel 3.2
-> 2.8), full-range RMS measures 1.237 codes against the corrected target,
and 240p moire improved from 5.00 to 4.50.

CRT Guest Advanced Fast was verified line by line to project onto MiSTer's
fixed pipeline identically to CRT Guest Advanced at defaults (same H window
after tap clamping, same sw0 scanline evaluator, same CGWG mask and
deconvergence chain; its glow source is a different blur texture with the
same flat-field value). The fitter consequently reproduces the same
coefficients under the Fast family's file names; the equivalence is a
validated contract in tools/targets/guest_fast_ref.py.

CRT Guest Advanced Fastest is a genuinely different response: its single
final pass has no glow lift, no preserve-scanline (pr_scan) multiply and no
w3-epsilon dimming, so peak white maps to exactly 255 and the overall
transfer sits slightly below the advanced one. It receives its own LUT, V
tables and mask fit (1.160 codes RMS, the closest Guest fit in the pack).


LOTTES STRICT-ORIGIN CORRECTION
-------------------------------
Lottes' source mask computes its selected channel from
`px + 3*py + 2 (mod 6)`. At output pixel (0,0), the source therefore begins on
green. A cyclically rolled 6x2 tile has identical averages and looks equivalent
over a full screen, but it is not pixel-identical at a declared origin.

The canonical mask is now anchored to the source phase:

  row 0: 27e,27e,17e,17e,47e,47e
  row 1: 17e,47e,47e,27e,27e,17e

This preserves the gain-compensated Lottes factorization while removing the
strict-origin phase error. Landscape and TATE use this same tile; MiSTer's
rotated mask mode performs the orientation change.


KNOWN IRREDUCIBLE DIFFERENCES
-----------------------------
Easymode: nonlinear inner-tap anti-ringing clamp, shader luma-assisted adaptive
control and automatic arbitrary source-height branching.

Guest Advanced (all three variants): chroma-dependent beam behavior and
spatial effects beyond one shared max-RGB adaptive scalar; the glow blur's
spatial spread (absent entirely in Fastest).

Royale: nonseparable multi-pass bloom, full 24x24 slot geometry, convergence /+diffusion effects and nonlinear local behavior.

Royale Kurozumi: Royale's structural limits plus independent PVM phosphor
channel multipliers that a single v2 token cannot express.

Lottes: true linear-light filtering and mask multiplication, nonseparable
Horz3/Horz5/Horz7 plus Tri/Bloom kernels, curvature and off-screen sampling.

The pack reports these boundaries because they are hardware limits, not fitting
parameters. Within the available MiSTer pipeline, the canonical files are the
single measured choices for the stated 1080p target.


REPRODUCIBILITY
---------------
The deterministic model, fitters and validators live in tools/. Source and RTL
revisions are pinned in SOURCES.txt. Generated families can be rebuilt with:

  python tools/build_port.py guest
  python tools/build_port.py guest_fast
  python tools/build_port.py guest_fastest
  python tools/build_port.py royale
  python tools/build_port.py kurozumi
  python tools/build_port.py easymode

Lottes uses its audited fixed tables and strict-origin mask directly.
