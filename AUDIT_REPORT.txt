====================================================================
 COLOUR ACCURACY AUDIT - 1080p v9
====================================================================

Audit date: 2026-07-30
Objective: the shader must not change the source game's colours.

All figures are produced by tools/validate_port.py, which evaluates uniform
source fields through MiSTer's exact integer pipeline (gamma LUT -> horizontal
4-tap polyphase FIR -> 8-bit clamp -> vertical FIR, adaptive where the preset
says so -> 8-bit clamp -> v2 shadow mask -> 8-bit clamp) at 240p -> 1080p.
Nothing here is measured from hardware; see LIMITS OF THIS AUDIT at the end.


THE HEADLINE FINDING: THE PACK WAS MATCHING THE WRONG QUANTITY
-------------------------------------------------------------

A CRT filter replaces each source pixel with a block of output pixels (mask
phases x scanline phases).  The colour a viewer perceives is that block's
average *light*.  The previous calibration matched the block's average *8-bit
code* instead, and gated on it.

Those are not the same number.  The display transfer function is convex, so
bright samples in the block emit disproportionately more light than their codes
imply -- mean(EOTF(code)) > EOTF(mean(code)).  Matching the code average
therefore leaves the picture brighter and less saturated than the source, and
the size of the error depends on the colour, so hue shifts too.

Measured on the shipped files, a 172 grey:

  Family                 code-space mean    PERCEIVED       error
  CRT Lottes                       172.7        184.7        +12.7
  CRT Royale                       171.9        183.6        +11.6
  CRT Royale Kurozumi              171.7        183.3        +11.3

The old validator printed the middle column, saw 172, and passed.  The user
saw the right-hand column.  That is the whole discrepancy between "ALL GATES
PASS" and "the colour is changed too much".

Whole-palette perceived error as shipped:

  CRT Guest Advanced      RMSE 20.66   (no calibration at all - see below)
  CRT Lottes              RMSE 16.41
  CRT Royale              RMSE 13.96
  CRT Royale Kurozumi     RMSE 13.84

The fix is to fit each LUT against the light-linear spatial mean.  This is
robust to the exact display gamma: a curve fitted for sRGB scores 5.36 / 5.37 /
5.49 palette RMSE when judged under sRGB / pure 2.2 / pure 2.4.


SECOND FINDING: GUEST ADVANCED WAS NEVER CALIBRATED
---------------------------------------------------

CRT Guest Advanced was absent from tools/color_match.py and from every colour
gate.  Its mask attenuated (mean transmission 0.9375) and its gamma LUT
subtracted up to 14 codes on top of that.  A 172 grey arrived as 149 - a 23
code loss, the largest error in the pack, completely ungated.


THIRD FINDING: MASK THROUGHPUT WAS LEFT BELOW MAXIMUM
----------------------------------------------------

Guest sat at 0.9375 mean transmission and Royale at 1.2031, against a legal
maximum of 1.2708 for a one-channel-per-cell grille.  Raising throughput is a
pure win, which is worth stating because the intuition runs the other way: a
*weaker* mask is less accurate, not more, because it lowers total transmission
and with it the highlight ceiling.  Measured on Kurozumi's grille:

  selected/16   mask contrast   perceived RMSE   white ceiling
      31             0.348           11.26           224.6
      24             0.231           11.23           224.6
      20             0.143           12.87           220.4
      16             0.032           15.94           212.5

So there is no "accuracy versus mask strength" trade to make here.  Full
throughput is both the most accurate and the most visible setting.


FOURTH FINDING: THE ADAPTIVE BEAM SHIFTS HUE, STRUCTURALLY
----------------------------------------------------------

MiSTer's adaptive vertical filter picks its beam profile from max(R,G,B) of the
whole pixel, so all three channels are filtered with coefficients chosen by the
brightest channel.  A saturated colour therefore filters its dark channels with
a bright-beam profile.  No shared gamma LUT can undo this, because the error is
a function of the colour, not of the code.

CRT Lottes measures exactly 0 here, because it is the one family with a fixed
(non-adaptive) beam.  tools/validate_patterns.py has been reporting this all
along as "shared_max_delta".

Contracting a family's two adaptive endpoints toward their midpoint scales the
coupling down directly.  Guest was contracted 50% (worst drift 8.4 -> 3.9
codes) and Kurozumi 30% (worst hue tilt 7.0 -> 2.9 codes, and its moire
*improved*, 2.50 -> 2.31).  Neither loses scanline depth: Guest holds 20.8% and
Kurozumi 26.3% throughout.

Royale is the exception and keeps its full adaptive beam.  Its endpoints differ
by up to 153 coefficient units, and contracting them drives 240p -> 1080p moire
through the 7.65-code limit (0% -> 7.00, 30% -> 8.00, 50% -> 10.00).  Uneven
scanline thickness is a worse artefact than the 2 codes of hue tilt it would
recover, so Royale accepts the tilt.  This is why Royale is simultaneously the
most accurate family on average and the one with the largest single hue error.


FIFTH FINDING: A SUB-CODE NEUTRAL TINT THAT CANNOT BE CALIBRATED OUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The horizontal FIR truncates twice per phase, so a flat field returns a +/-1
code ripple across the scaler's 9 phases at 4.5x -- a flat 83 comes back as
{82, 83}, while a flat 154 is exact.  Where the mask period shares a factor
with 9, each channel's selected columns land on a different residue class of
those phases (R on {0,3,6}, G on {1,4,7}, B on {2,5,8}), whose means differ by
up to 1/3 code.

This predicts the measured neutral channel spans exactly: Guest 0.000 (mask
width 2 is coprime with 9, so every channel visits every phase), Lottes 0.104,
Royale 0.165, Kurozumi 0.765.  At 0.3% of full scale it is far below a visible
step, but it is a real floor - the gate is set at 1.0 code rather than 0.


RESULT
------

Perceived colour, whole RGB cube at 9 levels per channel (729 colours), for
every colour that the highlight ceiling does not clip:

  Family                 RMSE   max drift   max hue tilt   was (RMSE)
  CRT Lottes             0.39         0.9            1.2        16.41
  CRT Royale Kurozumi    0.88         2.4            2.9        13.84
  CRT Royale             1.80         8.6           10.5        13.96
  CRT Guest Advanced     2.05         5.3            5.3        20.66

A 172 grey now arrives at 171.5 - 172.0 on all four, in perceived light.  Below
the highlight ceiling the pack is effectively transparent to colour: Lottes and
Kurozumi are inside 2.4 codes everywhere, and Royale's single worst case is one
saturated colour against a bright channel.

The TATE presets transpose the pipeline but reuse the landscape LUT.  Verified
rather than assumed: unclipped RMSE 0.49 / 0.72 / 1.82 / 2.11 for Lottes,
Kurozumi, Royale, Guest -- within 0.3 codes of their landscape values.


WHAT STILL DIFFERS FROM THE SOURCE: THE HIGHLIGHT CEILING
---------------------------------------------------------

This is the one remaining visible difference, and it is structural.  A
modulated field cannot average to code 255: the v2 mask format caps
non-selected channels at 15/16, and the 8-bit stages have no headroom above
255, so there is nothing to borrow from.  Measured ceilings:

  CRT Royale             240.0     (-15)
  CRT Guest Advanced     238.7     (-16)
  CRT Royale Kurozumi    223.7     (-31)
  CRT Lottes             219.6     (-35)

Everything at or above that ceiling is compressed toward it.  Colour and hue
are preserved; peak brightness is not.

The only lever left is scanline depth, and unlike mask contrast this one is a
genuine trade against the look.  Measured, with the LUT refitted at each point:

  CRT Lottes            depth   ceiling   unclipped RMSE
    as shipped          10.5%     219.6         0.39
    50% flattened        5.1%     231.0         ~0.5
    fully flattened       0.2%     242.5         ~0.3

  CRT Royale Kurozumi   depth   ceiling   unclipped RMSE
    as shipped          26.3%     223.7         0.88
    50% flattened       12.6%     233.1         ~0.6
    fully flattened       0.0%     242.6         ~0.3

Both families were left at their shipped scanline depth: flattening them would
trade away the CRT look the presets exist to provide, and the residual it buys
back is a highlight ceiling, not a colour error.  To pick a different point,
set KUROZUMI_SCANLINE_BLEND in tools/color_match.py (or add the same blend for
Lottes), re-run it, and re-run tools/validate_port.py -- the white gates in
LIMITS will need raising to match.


CHANGES APPLIED
---------------

tools/color_match.py    Fits every LUT against the light-linear spatial mean
                        instead of the code-space mean.  Covers all four
                        families.  Raises each mask to its target throughput
                        while preserving cell geometry, and asserts equal R/G/B
                        mask energy rather than assuming it.  Adds per-family
                        adaptive-endpoint contraction.  Each V conditioning
                        step carries its own idempotency marker.
tools/validate_port.py  Gates on perceived colour, not code-space colour.  Adds
                        Guest.  Adds a 729-colour RGB-cube sweep with drift and
                        hue-tilt limits, because the 11-colour palette missed
                        Royale's worst case.  Reports the highlight ceiling as a
                        measured value and excludes clipped colours from the
                        fidelity metric.  Code-space RMSE is still printed, for
                        reference only.
Gamma/                  All four curves regenerated; CRT Lottes (Port).txt is
                        new (Lottes previously ran with gamma disabled, which
                        is why its 172 grey read 184.7).
Shadow_Masks/           Guest 0.9375 -> 1.09375 mean transmission; Royale
                        1.2031 -> 1.2708, flattening a +/-2/16 vertical ripple
                        on identical horizontal triads that was not a true
                        offset slot.  Lottes and Kurozumi were already at max.
Filters/                Guest V endpoints contracted 50%; Kurozumi V endpoints
                        contracted 30% (on top of its existing 65% scan-free
                        DC blend).
Presets/                CRT Lottes and CRT Lottes - TATE now reference the new
                        gamma file instead of gamma=off.
tools/preset_contracts.py, tools/selftest_fileio.py
                        Updated for Lottes' new gamma file (3 -> 4 gamma files).


LIMITS OF THIS AUDIT
--------------------

Every number is modelled, not captured.  The model (tools/mister_model.py) is
documented as verified against ascal.vhd / shadowmask.sv / video.cpp at pinned
commits, and reproduces MiSTer's truncation and saturation exactly, but it is
still a model.  The perceptual figures additionally assume the display applies
a standard sRGB EOTF; the gamma-sensitivity check above shows the fit barely
moves across sRGB / 2.2 / 2.4, so this assumption is not load-bearing.

Not verified on hardware.  The remaining check worth doing is a capture from
the real MiSTer at 1080p of a known test pattern, compared against the
unfiltered output, to confirm the modelled ceilings and the 172-grey match.

The audit covers uniform fields.  It says nothing about how each preset treats
edges, ringing, or bloom, which are sharpness properties rather than colour
properties; tools/validate_patterns.py covers those separately.
